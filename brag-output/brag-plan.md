# Brag Plan: Waypoint Dispute Console (Ryde Digital Native Track)

## What is this app?
A multi-agent dispute resolver: a Rider Advocate and a Driver Advocate argue from the same sealed evidence, a Judge rules, and every exchange is visible in the Communication Log.

## The angle
A small-claims court broadcast, played perfectly straight. The joke and the claim are the same thing: a $5.00 argument gets three AI agents, a sealed evidence set and a written ruling, in about the time it takes to read this. The product's own line carries it: "Watch three agents settle a dispute — in the open."

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black screen, typed out: "10:04. Driver cancels." then "Rider charged $5.00 for a no-show." The viewer's question: was that fair? (Real case DISP-003: driver waited 4 minutes, free wait is 5.)

## Key moments (the middle)
- Console hero headline lands with its three agents lit: Rider Advocate, Driver Advocate, Judge.
- The Communication Log fills line by line (real wording): evidence sealed, policy retrieved, a Submission from each advocate, the Judge's assessment.
- Ruling card stamps in: uphold, fee reversal SGD 5.00, Confidence 0.80, final.
- Same agents, opposite result: DISP-002 rejected, then DISP-008 partial refund SGD 5.39 (code computed, not model-guessed).

## Outro / punchline
"Refund recommended. Never executed." then the Waypoint mark and "A clearer way forward."

## User flow worth showing
Entry: pick a dispute (DISP-003) → Key action: Communication Log fills as advocates argue and the Judge assesses → Result: Ruling tab with outcome, amount, Confidence, and "What each person is told".

## Tone
- Preset: deadpan
- Creative direction: small-claims court broadcast, played completely straight
- Interpretation: calm holds, one idea per beat, dry on-screen type; the absurdity of three agents on a $5.00 fee does the work.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 22s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #0D1218 (charcoal page and navigation)
- Accent: #315CF5 (primary actions); links on light #2851DC
- Text: #17202D on white surfaces; #AEB9C9 secondary on dark
- Display font: Nunito (local variable font, prototypes/shared/fonts/nunito-variable.ttf)
- Body font: Nunito, falling back to Segoe UI / system sans-serif
- Strongest visual element: the console's white work surfaces on charcoal, the workflow canvas with three lit agents, and the Communication Log. Status is always words plus colour. Pip (compass mascot) and the route-shaped W mark are original SVGs.
- Fictional data note: trip and dispute IDs are the project's mock data (no real people). Rider and driver have no names on screen.

## Share copy (draft)
Three AI agents, one $5.00 no-show fee, zero humans in the loop, and every word of the argument on screen. Waypoint's dispute console, built for the Ryde track.

## Audio direction
- Role: sparse professional accents over a low warm bed
- Music: one of the bundled "happy-beats-business-moves" tracks, quiet; pick the calmest at composition time
- Music treatment: starts under the hook at low volume, lifts slightly at the ruling stamp, fades out under the final mark
- Music cue guidance: read the chosen track's preset in assets/music/cues/ if present; use 1-2 strong cues (the ruling stamp, the "opposite ruling" beat). Beat windows only for the log lines, which must stay readable (see hold note).
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; bass energy gently lifts the glow on the lit agent nodes, no waveforms
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched
- Audio-coupled moments: hook typed with soft key ticks; log lines land with a quiet tick; ruling stamp gets one dry thud
- Restraint rule: deadpan means no whooshes, no risers, no stings; silence between beats is fine.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The fee — 3.5s
Charcoal screen. Typed in Nunito: "10:04. Driver cancels." then "Rider charged $5.00 for a no-show." Each line holds about 1.2s settled.
Sequential/interaction: yes — two lines type out one after the other.
Audio intent: dry, quiet curiosity.
Audio-coupled idea: soft key ticks while typing.
Music: bed enters very low.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The console — 4.5s
The console header and hero headline "Watch three agents settle a dispute — in the open." The workflow canvas below shows three nodes lighting up one after another: Rider Advocate, Driver Advocate, Judge. Headline holds about 2s settled; node labels about 0.8s each.
Sequential/interaction: yes — three agent nodes light one by one.
Audio intent: the scene gets confident.
Audio-coupled idea: a quiet card tick per node.
Music: bed settles in.
Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The argument — 7s
The Communication Log fills with real lines, hold the final set on screen: "Evidence sealed" → "8 applicable Policy Clause(s) retrieved." → "Submission filed: Reverse the no-show cancellation fee…" (Rider Advocate) → "Submission filed: …accepting reversal of the prematurely applied no-show fee." (Driver Advocate) → Judge: "Ruling (final): uphold, fee_reversal SGD 5.00." Then the Ruling card stamps in: uphold · SGD 5.00 · Confidence 0.80. Log lines reveal fast (about 0.5s apart) but the full set holds about 2s afterwards; no line leaves before it was readable.
Sequential/interaction: yes — log lines append one by one, ruling card stamps in last.
Audio intent: procedure, then a verdict.
Audio-coupled idea: tick per log line; one dry thud on the stamp (strong cue).
Music: lifts slightly at the stamp.
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Same agents, opposite ruling — 4.5s
Split into three stacked ruling chips, one at a time: DISP-003 "uphold · SGD 5.00", DISP-002 "reject · no action", DISP-008 "partially uphold · SGD 5.39 (computed in code)". Caption: "Same agents. Same policy. The evidence decides." Each chip holds about 1s, caption about 1.5s.
Sequential/interaction: yes — three chips appear one by one.
Audio intent: dry amusement.
Audio-coupled idea: card tick per chip.
Music: steady.
Transition mood: slow crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Never executed — 2.5s
Waypoint mark, "Refund recommended. Never executed." then the tagline "A clearer way forward." Held, music fades out.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: quiet landing.
Audio-coupled idea: none
Music: fades under the mark.
Transition mood: end

**Music mood for this video:** deadpan, warm and restrained
**Audio summary:** a quiet bed, soft ticks while text types and log lines land, one dry thud on the ruling, then a fade-out.
