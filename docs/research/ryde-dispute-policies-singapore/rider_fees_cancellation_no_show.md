# Ryde (Singapore) rider-facing fare, cancellation, no-show and waiting-time policies

Research date: 9 October 2026. Singapore only (Ryde Group Ltd / Ryde Technologies Pte. Ltd., rydesharing.com).

Method notes for the report writer:
- Ryde's help centre (help.rydesharing.com, Zendesk) returns HTTP 403 to the normal page fetcher. The article text below was read verbatim from the help centre's public Zendesk JSON API (`https://help.rydesharing.com/api/v2/help_center/en-us/articles.json`, 325 articles), so quotes are exact. Citations use each article's public `html_url`.
- Dates: Zendesk exposes `edited_at` (last content edit) and `updated_at` (last any-change, including metadata). Both are given as "edited / updated". Where they differ, `edited_at` is the better indicator of when the wording last changed.
- All "$" amounts on Ryde pages are SGD (Ryde writes "$", not "S$").
- Comparator material (Grab, Gojek, TADA) is in clearly labelled "COMPARATOR" blocks and is never Ryde's rule. Grab's and TADA's own help centres could not be fetched; that material is second-hand and flagged.
- Not consulted (ran out of budget): Ryde Group SEC filings (F-1, 20-F), App Store / Google Play listing text, Straits Times / CNA coverage.

## What is Ryde's cancellation policy for riders: fee amounts, the grace period after booking or after a driver is matched, and differences by product?

### Takeaway
Ryde's current rider-facing policy charges a flat Cancellation Fee of $6.61 if the rider cancels more than 3 minutes after being matched (on-demand), or inside the 15-minutes-before-scheduled-time window for advance bookings; cancellations within 3 minutes of matching are free. The policy applies to every service except RydeTAXI, with no per-product fee differences published; older Ryde pages still show $4.50, and 2021 press shows $4.30 / 4 minutes.

### Inferences
- The 3-minute clock runs from the time of match, not from driver arrival; Ryde says the grace period is "guaranteed", so an early-arriving driver does not shorten it.
- For an advance booking matched inside the last 15 minutes, the rider gets the on-demand 3-minute grace instead.
- Ryde publishes one flat cancellation fee across RydeX, RydeXL, RydeFLASH, RydePET, RydeLUXE and RydePOOL; no product-specific amounts were found.
- The rider-facing $6.61 and the driver-facing $4.50 probably coexist (rider pays fee plus platform and transaction fees; driver receives $4.50), but Ryde does not publish a breakdown that adds up to $6.61, so this is unconfirmed.

### Gaps
- RydeHITCH: no article in the Ryde help centre's 325-article index mentions RydeHITCH by name. Ryde publishes nothing specific on RydeHITCH cancellation.
- No Ryde rule was found that waives the fee when the driver is late versus ETA or is not moving toward the rider (Gojek and, historically, Grab publish one; see comparators).
- No Ryde statement on whether a rider can be charged both a cancellation fee and a waiting fee on the same booking.
- Ryde's Terms of Use point to "the Cancellation and Waiting Time Policy on Ryde's website", but the website post (Oct 12, 2023) contains no dollar amounts; the amounts live only in help-centre articles.
- Grab's and TADA's current official help-centre wording could not be retrieved (Grab help pages failed to load; no tada.global page surfaced).

## What is Ryde's no-show policy: how long must a driver wait at the pickup point before a rider can be marked a no-show, what fee is charged, and who receives it?

### Takeaway
A driver must press "I'm Here", be at the pick-up location per GPS, and wait more than 3 minutes; if the driver then cancels, the rider is charged the $6.61 Cancellation Fee as a "Rider NO SHOW". Ryde says the fee compensates the driver and is credited to the driver's wallet, but the driver-side page states the driver receives $4.50.

### Cited Findings
- No-show trigger (on-demand): "The driver presses "I'm Here", waits for > 3 minutes for the rider to arrive at the pick-up point and cancels the trip (Rider NO SHOW and no trip completion)" leads to "a Cancellation Fee of $6.61*" (edited/updated 2026-02-26) — [Ryde Help: Cancellation and Waiting Fee](https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/23534478435353-Cancellation-and-Waiting-Fee)
- No-show trigger (advance): "The driver cancels the trip after waiting for > 3 minutes for the Rider to arrive at the pick-up point (Rider NO SHOW and no trip completion)" — [Ryde Help: Cancellation and Waiting Fee](https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/23534478435353-Cancellation-and-Waiting-Fee)
- Advance trips, website wording: charges apply if "You don't show up for more than 3 mins after the scheduled time." (October 12, 2023) — [Ryde: Cancellation & Waiting Time Policy](https://rydesharing.com/everything-you-need-to-know-about-our-new-cancellation-waiting-time-policy/)
- Evidence conditions: "Drivers are only allowed to indicate arrival and impose the Waiting Time Fee only if he/she: Is at the pick-up location according to GPS data AND Has waited for more than 3 mins. Otherwise, you will not be charged the $6.61 Waiting Time Fee if any of the above are not fulfilled. Nonetheless, do reach out to us if you feel you have been charged unfairly or wrongly, and we'll be happy to assist you." (edited/updated 2026-02-26) — [Ryde Help: What if my driver hasn't arrived, but has indicated that he/she arrived?](https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4407754403353-What-if-my-driver-hasn-t-arrived-but-has-indicated-that-he-she-arrived)
- Who receives it (rider-facing, cash trips): "If the trip is cancelled by the rider or the driver according to the conditions in our Cancellation and Waiting Time Policy, the Cancellation or Waiting Time Fee will be deducted from your RydeCoins wallet and credited directly to the driver's wallet." (edited 2023-05-04 / updated 2025-11-23) — [Ryde Help: How am I charged under the Policy if I am paying my fare by Cash?](https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408070775193-How-am-I-charged-under-the-Policy-if-I-am-paying-my-fare-by-Cash)

Cancellations from no-shows will also be factored into the driver's account." If you have been charged for a trip that you have not taken, kindly share the details with us to investigate the matter." COMPARATOR (not Ryde):
- Gojek Singapore: $4 if "Your driver cancels 4 minutes after arriving at your pick-up location"; "The 4-minute countdown does not include any time that your driver is still completing a previous trip" (23 Jul 2024) — [Gojek SG blog](https://www.gojek.com/sg/blog/news-cancellation-fee-gojek-singapore-updates)
- Gojek driver page (via search summary): after 4 minutes of waiting a driver can choose to receive either a cancellation fee or waiting fees — [Gojek SG driver blog](https://www.gojek.com/sg/blog/dp-waiting-fee)
- Grab Singapore (2019 news, likely outdated): S$4 for no-show; drivers can cancel freely on passengers who take more than five minutes to show up; a passenger can be charged either a no-show or a late-cancellation fee, not both; drivers get 100% of the fees — [Vulcan Post](https://vulcanpost.com/657902/grab-singapore-cancellation-policy/)

### Inferences
- Ryde's three published no-show evidence elements are: (1) driver tapped "I'm Here", (2) driver GPS at the pick-up location, (3) more than 3 minutes elapsed before the driver cancelled. These map directly onto checks a dispute system can run on trip logs.
- Ryde's pages are inconsistent on the label and amount: the rider policy calls a no-show a $6.61 Cancellation Fee and the completed-trip late-boarding charge a $5 Waiting Time Fee, while the "driver hasn't arrived" article calls it "the $6.61 Waiting Time Fee" and the older driver article calls a no-show a $4.50 Waiting Time Fee.
- For advance bookings the 3-minute wait appears to run from the scheduled time (website wording), not from an early arrival.

### Gaps
- Ryde does not publish how "at the pick-up location according to GPS data" is measured (no radius or tolerance).
- Ryde does not say whether the driver must attempt to call or message the rider before cancelling as a no-show.
- The split of the rider's $6.61 between driver and Ryde is not published.
- How a no-show fee is collected from a cash rider with an empty RydeCoins wallet is not stated.

## What is the free waiting time and the per-minute or per-block waiting charge after it?

### Takeaway
Ryde gives 3 minutes of free waiting after the driver presses "I'm Here", then a single flat Waiting Time Fee of $5 if the rider boards and the trip is completed. Ryde publishes no per-minute or per-block waiting charge and no cap; the fee is added by the driver at drop-off.

### Cited Findings
- "Riders may be charged a Waiting Time Fee of $5 at the end of the trip if: The driver presses "I'm Here", waits for > 3 minutes for the rider to board, and continues the trip (Rider boards and driver completes trip)." (same wording for on-demand; for advance trips: "The driver waits for > 3 minutes for the Rider to board and continues to complete the trip") (edited/updated 2026-02-26) — [Ryde Help: Cancellation and Waiting Fee](https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/23534478435353-Cancellation-and-Waiting-Fee)
- "A Waiting Time Fee allows our Driver-Partners to be compensated by riders for the time spent waiting at a pickup location. Waiting Time Fees begin to kick in after the driver waits for more than 3 minutes at the pick-up point for the rider to board." (edited 2023-01-01 / updated 2025-11-23) — [Ryde Help: Why am I charged for a Waiting Time Fee?](https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4407754351257-Why-am-I-charged-for-a-Waiting-Time-Fee)

### Inferences
- Ryde's waiting charge is binary (0 or $5), triggered at >3 minutes, and depends on the driver tapping a button at drop-off; it is not system-computed from elapsed time.
- Because the same "I'm Here" + GPS + >3 minutes conditions gate both the waiting fee and the no-show fee, the only difference is the outcome: trip completed ($5 waiting fee) versus driver cancels ($6.61 cancellation fee).

### Gaps
- Ryde is silent on per-minute or per-block waiting charges, a waiting-fee cap, and a maximum time a driver must wait before cancelling.
- Ryde is silent on waiting at intermediate stops on multi-stop trips.
- Whether the rider-paid $5 passes in full to the driver is not stated.

## Are Ryde fares upfront/fixed or metered? What does Ryde say happens to the fare if the driver takes a longer route, if the rider changes destination, or adds a stop?

### Takeaway
Ryde fares are fixed upfront on the pick-up and drop-off points for every service except RydeTAXI (metered, per taxi company). A mid-trip destination change or added stop needs driver acceptance, recalculates the fare and adds a $3.00 add/edit destination fee; Ryde publishes nothing about fare adjustment or review when a driver takes a longer route.

### Cited Findings
- "Fares quoted for our services are generally fixed, except for RydeTAXI. However, please note that there may be additional charges such as tolls that could occur during your trip. For a detailed breakdown of your fare, please refer to your receipt. All fees and surcharges will be clearly listed for your reference." (edited 2025-07-17 / updated 2025-11-23) — [Ryde Help: I was overcharged for ERP/toll](https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/30741244296217-I-was-overcharged-for-ERP-toll)

- An added stop mid-trip is treated the same as a destination edit (one "$3.00 add/edit destination fee"), and only once the driver accepts within roughly 60 seconds; a declined or timed-out request leaves the original fare unchanged.

## How are ERP/tolls, platform fees, peak or late-night surcharges and other add-ons applied?

### Takeaway
