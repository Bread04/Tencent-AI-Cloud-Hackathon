## 1. Route, detours, unauthorised stops, extra passengers, and pickup-point duties before cancelling / marking a no-show

### Takeaway
Ryde's published driver material is thin on route conduct: it requires going "directly" to the pickup and treats a rider's preferred route as something to agree before the trip, but publishes no rule on following the app route, detours, unauthorised stops or carrying other passengers. The only concrete pickup rule is procedural: press "I'm Here", wait more than 3 minutes, then cancel as a no-show.

### Cited Findings
- RYDE [FETCHED], Code of Conduct (no last-updated date; footer "© 2026 Ryde Technologies Pte. Ltd."), driver duty en route to pickup: "Please travel directly to the rider's pick-up location once you have accepted the trip." — [Ryde Code of Conduct](https://rydesharing.com/code-of-conduct/)
- RYDE [FETCHED], same page, driver contact duty: "Drop your passenger a message to let them know you are on your way along with your estimated time of arrival." — [Ryde Code of Conduct](https://rydesharing.com/code-of-conduct/)
- RYDE [FETCHED], same page, route preference is put on the passenger and framed as mutual agreement: passengers with requests such as a preferred route should "communicate this with the driver and come to a mutual agreement prior to starting the trip." — [Ryde Code of Conduct](https://rydesharing.com/code-of-conduct/)
- RYDE [FETCHED], same page, rider duty at pickup: "Be prepared to wait for the driver at the pick-up location once you have matched with a driver", tied to avoiding "the Cancellation and Waiting Time fees." — [Ryde Code of Conduct](https://rydesharing.com/code-of-conduct/)
- RYDE [FETCHED], same page: "Contacting your passenger after the trip has ended is strictly prohibited." and "Physical and verbal assault or harassment is not tolerated on our platform." — [Ryde Code of Conduct](https://rydesharing.com/code-of-conduct/)
- RYDE [FETCHED]: the Code of Conduct page does not address detours, stops, extra passengers, mess/damage, lost items, fraud, or disciplinary action. — [Ryde Code of Conduct](https://rydesharing.com/code-of-conduct/)
- RYDE [SNIPPET], no-show procedure: a Waiting Time Fee of $4.50 applies when the driver has pressed "I'm Here", waits more than 3 minutes, and then cancels because the rider never arrived; it also applies when the driver waits more than 3 minutes and the rider then boards (fee added at end of trip). — [Ryde Help: Cancellation and Waiting Time Policy](https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/33362932998937-Cancellation-and-Waiting-Time-Policy)
- RYDE [FETCHED], Driver Handbook 2024 ("Last updated: 21st December 2023"), no-show cancellations: "In the case of rider no-shows, drivers can still cancel the trip. Drivers do not have to worry about account suspension from trip cancellations." — [Ryde Driver-Partners Handbook 2024 (PDF)](https://rydesharing.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Ryde-Driver-Partners-Handbook-2024.pdf)
- RYDE [FETCHED], Handbook best practice (advice, not a rule): "Stay updated on the best and fastest routes to take". — [Ryde Driver-Partners Handbook 2024 (PDF)](https://rydesharing.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Ryde-Driver-Partners-Handbook-2024.pdf)
- RYDE [FETCHED], Handbook, the closest thing to an "other passengers / ghost trip" rule is the fraud definition: "Drivers who are found to have overcharged riders and/or completed the trip without the rider on board may be liable to account suspension. The Ryde driver app has Artificial Intelligence (AI) algorithms that will automatically detect suspicious driver activity." — [Ryde Driver-Partners Handbook 2024 (PDF)](https://rydesharing.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Ryde-Driver-Partners-Handbook-2024.pdf)
- RYDE [SNIPPET], "irregular trip behaviour" SMS/email goes to drivers whose accounts an algorithm has flagged; listed example triggers include multiple cancellations, low ratings, overcharging extra fees, misuse of the cancellation or waiting-time button, trips completed without a rider on board, unusual trip patterns, and speeding or reckless driving. — [Ryde Help: irregular trip behaviour](https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/6959605073177-I-received-an-SMS-or-Email-regarding-irregular-trip-behaviour-What-does-that-mean)
- RYDE [FETCHED], Terms of Use (page states "Last modified: 6th April 2022"), Partner undertaking: "You shall not contact the User for purposes other than in connection with the Service;" and RydePOOL: "shall not, in any circumstances, solicit for any User on a road" and "You agree that You shall not exceed two (2) paid carpool trips in a day." The fetch found no clause on route in the Terms. — [Ryde Terms of Use](https://rydesharing.com/terms-of-use/)
- RYDE [FETCHED], multi-stop and pooled trips are legitimate product features, so an extra stop or co-rider is not per se a violation: Handbook lists RydePOOL "1 passenger only / Drop-off sequence dependent on route" and RydeSEND "Users can add up multiple stops"; the 7 Driver Commitments page lists a "multi-stops feature". — [Ryde Driver-Partners Handbook 2024 (PDF)](https://rydesharing.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Ryde-Driver-Partners-Handbook-2024.pdf); [Ryde 7 Driver Commitments](https://rydesharing.com/7-driver-commitments/)
- **COMPARATOR (Grab, not Ryde)** [FETCHED], Driver Code of Conduct (no last-updated date): "Discuss and agree with the user before taking an alternative route"; listed infringement "Making an unnecessary detour (e.g. to fill fuel)"; "You shall not have another person or pet inside your vehicle while you are on the Grab platform."; "Communicate with and wait for your passenger at the correct pick-up point"; "Wait for your user for a reasonable amount of time."; infringement "Cancelling a booking without a valid reason or giving a false reason". — [Grab SG Driver Code of Conduct](https://www.grab.com/sg/terms-policies/code-of-conduct-driver/)

### Inferences
- For mock clauses, the defensible Ryde-specific route position is "driver goes directly to pickup; any non-default route needs rider agreement before the trip starts". A rule that the driver must follow the in-app suggested route is NOT something Ryde publishes; it would have to be modelled on Grab's wording and labelled as such.
- "Unusual trip patterns" and "trips completed without a rider on board" in the irregular-trip article are the only Ryde hooks for route-deviation or wrong-passenger disputes, and they are algorithmic flags rather than stated conduct rules.
- Ryde requires a message to the rider on the way to pickup, but nothing fetched says the driver must call or message the rider at the pickup point before cancelling as a no-show; the gating conditions found are the "I'm Here" press and the 3-minute wait.

### Gaps
- Ryde publishes no rule (in any source reached) on: following the app's suggested route, detours, unauthorised stops, or picking up non-booked passengers. Explicitly silent.
- The help-centre version of the Code of Conduct ([article 15395890865561](https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/15395890865561-Ryde-Code-Of-Conduct)) could not be opened (HTTP 403); it may contain more than the rydesharing.com page.
- Whether a contact attempt is required before a no-show cancellation is not confirmed.
- The Grab Code of Conduct fetch also returned penalty-table text referring to "LTFRB" (a Philippine regulator), so that page's disciplinary table may not be Singapore-specific; only the conduct sentences above were used.

## 2. Driver-side cancellation rules and penalties

### Takeaway
Ryde's published threshold is a cancellation rate above 17.5% (alongside a driver rating below 4.80) as a condition where an account is "liable for suspension", with no stated suspension length; reactivation costs a fee. The same document also tells drivers they will not be suspended for cancellations if they keep accepting trips, so the rule is soft and internally inconsistent.

### Cited Findings
- RYDE [FETCHED], Handbook 2024 (last updated 21 Dec 2023), "Conditions where driver accounts will be liable for suspension": "Driver Rating (DR) < 4.80"; "Cancellation Rate (CR) > 17.5%"; "Adverse Ratings — Drivers who receive poor ratings for errant behaviour. Eg. racist, ethnicity, national origin, disability, etc."; "Min. Acceptance Rate (AR) — We do not have a minimum AR criteria"; "Fraudulent Activity — Drivers who overcharge riders and/or complete the trip without the rider on board"; "Dormant Account — Did not complete at least one trip within a 12 month period". (The PDF's table columns are misaligned in text extraction; pairings here follow the evident meaning.) — [Ryde Driver-Partners Handbook 2024 (PDF)](https://rydesharing.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Ryde-Driver-Partners-Handbook-2024.pdf)
- RYDE [FETCHED], Handbook, how CR works: "The driver's cancellation rate will be calculated based on his/her most recent trips. The driver's cancellation rate will increase after a driver accepts a trip booking and cancels. We strongly advise drivers to only accept jobs that they can fulfill." — [Ryde Driver-Partners Handbook 2024 (PDF)](https://rydesharing.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Ryde-Driver-Partners-Handbook-2024.pdf)
- RYDE [FETCHED], Handbook, the softening sentence: "Our app algorithms will look at the driver's most recent trips. As long as the driver continues accepting trips, he/she will not be suspended from cancellations." — [Ryde Driver-Partners Handbook 2024 (PDF)](https://rydesharing.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Ryde-Driver-Partners-Handbook-2024.pdf)
- RYDE [FETCHED], Handbook, rating mechanics: "The driver's rating is calculated based on his/her last 100 trips"; "All new drivers will have a new default rating of 4.90 and will be adjusted after the 11th unique review. Please allow up to 7 working days for the update to be reflected." — [Ryde Driver-Partners Handbook 2024 (PDF)](https://rydesharing.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Ryde-Driver-Partners-Handbook-2024.pdf)
- RYDE [SNIPPET], the current help-centre "Driver Performance and Community Standards" article shows the same thresholds ("Driver Rating (DR) · < 4.80 Cancellation Rate (CR) · > 17.5%"). — [Ryde Help: Driver Performance and Community Standards](https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/20244107265689-Driver-Performance-and-Community-Standards); a second article with the same title exists at [33362721809049](https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/33362721809049-Driver-Performance-and-Community-Standards)
- RYDE [SNIPPET], older undated "The Driver's Handbook" PDF gives different numbers: cancellation threshold "exceeds 17.0%", rating reset to 4.70, a $25 reactivation fee, and inactivity after 6 months. Likely superseded. — [Ryde older Driver's Handbook (PDF)](https://www.rydesharing.com/wp-content/uploads/Thedrivershandbook.pdf)
- RYDE [SNIPPET], cancellation FAQ: "your profile will be flagged for frequent cancellations and may even be suspended if your cancellation rating is too high". — [Ryde Help: What are the penalties for cancellation?](https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001439673-What-are-the-penalties-for-cancellation)
- RYDE [FETCHED], blog post dated 12 October 2023: drivers with a high cancellation rate may face suspension and may be ineligible for driver bonuses and incentives (reported by the fetch as a summary, not a verbatim quote). — [Ryde: Cancellation & Waiting Time Policy explainer](https://rydesharing.com/everything-you-need-to-know-about-our-new-cancellation-waiting-time-policy/)
- RYDE [FETCHED], Terms of Use cl. 7.1 (page says "Last modified: 6th April 2022"): "You agree that high and/or frequent cancellation rates will impair the Users' experience"; "the cancellation shall be based on acceptable cancellation reasons as shown in the Application"; "A cancellation that is not based on one of the acceptable reasons or ignoring a booking may be counted" (the search excerpt completes this as "...in determining if your access to the Service will be temporarily suspended"); "Ryde reserves the right to amend the acceptable cancellation reasons from time to time." — [Ryde Terms of Use](https://rydesharing.com/terms-of-use/)
- RYDE [SNIPPET], a RydePET-specific article exists titled "What happens if I cancel after accepting due to the pet?"; its content was not retrieved. — [Ryde Help article 49718401889561](https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/49718401889561-What-happens-if-I-cancel-after-accepting-due-to-the-pet)
- THIRD-PARTY: in one reported case a rider was charged a $6.50 cancellation fee and Ryde said it had launched an internal review and temporarily suspended the driver; in another, a rider charged $4.30 after the driver cancelled was refunded by Ryde after review. — [MustShareNews: Ryde passenger made to pay despite trip being cancelled by driver](https://mustsharenews.com/ryde-passenger-cancelled-trip/); [The Independent Singapore](https://theindependent.sg/?p=675776) (the second link was surfaced by search only and not opened)
- **COMPARATOR (Grab, not Ryde)** [FETCHED]: "Extreme cancellation of 50% and above (All driver-partners)"; "Daily High cancellation of 25% and above (Food, Mart and Express driver-partners only)". — [Grab SG Driver Code of Conduct](https://www.grab.com/sg/terms-policies/code-of-conduct-driver/)

### Inferences
- Ryde publishes a threshold but not a consequence ladder: no stated suspension duration, no stated number of strikes, no per-cancellation monetary penalty on the driver. The only money consequence of suspension is the reactivation fee (section 7) and loss of bonus eligibility.
- Because the Terms tie counted cancellations to "acceptable cancellation reasons as shown in the Application", a dispute system should treat the driver's selected in-app cancellation reason as a key evidence field. The list of acceptable reasons itself is not published.

### Gaps
- The list of "acceptable cancellation reasons" is in-app only; not found in any public source.
- No published suspension duration for cancellation-rate breaches; no published number of trips in the "most recent trips" window for CR.
- Help-centre article text and update dates unverified (HTTP 403).

## 3. What a driver receives when a rider cancels late or does not show up

### Takeaway
Ryde's help centre states a $4.50 Cancellation Fee and a $4.50 Waiting Time Fee, each triggered after a 3-minute window (15 minutes before pickup for advance bookings), credited to the driver. The $4.50 figures come from search excerpts only and news reports cite other amounts ($4.30, $6.50), so treat the dollar value as needing confirmation.

### Cited Findings
- RYDE [SNIPPET], on-demand trips: "All cancellations made within 3 minutes of matching are not chargeable"; after that window the driver receives a Cancellation Fee of $4.50. — [Ryde Help: Cancellation and Waiting Time Policy](https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/33362932998937-Cancellation-and-Waiting-Time-Policy); an earlier article with the same title is at [20244064619161](https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/20244064619161-Cancellation-and-Waiting-Time-Policy)
- RYDE [SNIPPET], Waiting Time Fee of $4.50 in two cases: (a) driver pressed "I'm Here", waited more than 3 minutes, then cancelled because the rider never arrived; (b) driver waited more than 3 minutes, rider boarded and the trip went ahead, with the fee added at the end of the trip. — [Ryde Help: Cancellation and Waiting Time Policy](https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/33362932998937-Cancellation-and-Waiting-Time-Policy)
- RYDE [SNIPPET], advance (scheduled) trips: "all cancellations made more than 15 minutes before scheduled trip time are not chargeable". — [Ryde Help: Cancellation and Waiting Time Policy](https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/33362932998937-Cancellation-and-Waiting-Time-Policy)
- RYDE [FETCHED], blog post dated 12 October 2023 (no dollar amounts on the page; may be superseded on details): fees apply to all Ryde services except RydeTAXI; the cancellation fee compensates drivers for travel to the pickup and the waiting time fee for time spent waiting; on-demand charges apply if the rider does not show up, if the driver waits more than 3 minutes, or if the rider cancels more than 3 minutes after matching; advance-trip charges apply if the rider does not show up more than 3 minutes after the scheduled time or cancels less than 15 minutes before the scheduled time after a match. — [Ryde: Cancellation & Waiting Time Policy explainer](https://rydesharing.com/everything-you-need-to-know-about-our-new-cancellation-waiting-time-policy/)
- RYDE [SNIPPET], payment mechanics: for cashless trips the fee is taken from the rider's RydeCoins wallet and credited to the driver; for cash trips the rider pays the final fare shown in the app including the waiting fee. Separate driver articles cover keying in the fee for cash trips. — [Ryde Help: How am I charged under the Policy if I am paying by Cash](https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408070775193-How-am-I-charged-under-the-Policy-if-I-am-paying-my-fare-by-Cash); [Ryde Help: How do I charge the Cancellation or Waiting Time Fee if the Rider pays by Cash](https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408071485081-How-do-I-charge-the-Cancellation-or-Waiting-Time-Fee-if-the-Rider-pays-by-Cash)
- RYDE [FETCHED], Terms of Use cl. 7.2: a rider who cancels or does "not show up at the designated location" faces "you may be charged a Cancellation Fee or such other Cancellation Fee as Ryde may notify"; riders who think they were wrongly charged can contact Ryde and refunds are at Ryde's discretion (the latter as summarised by the fetch). — [Ryde Terms of Use](https://rydesharing.com/terms-of-use/)
- RYDE [FETCHED], Terms of Use cl. 7.1: a driver who feels unfairly rated "due to the additional fees imposed on riders" can ask Ryde to "consider removing ratings that are irrelevant to the driver's quality of service" under the Ratings and Review Moderation Policy. — [Ryde Terms of Use](https://rydesharing.com/terms-of-use/); policy article at [Ryde Help: Ratings and Reviews Moderation Policy](https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/20244161586585-Ratings-and-Reviews-Moderation-Policy) (not opened)
- RYDE [SNIPPET], "misuse of the cancellation or waiting-time button" is a listed trigger for the irregular-trip-behaviour flag. — [Ryde Help: irregular trip behaviour](https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/6959605073177-I-received-an-SMS-or-Email-regarding-irregular-trip-behaviour-What-does-that-mean)
- THIRD-PARTY: reported rider charges of $4.30 and $6.50 in individual disputed cases, both reviewed by Ryde (one refunded, one leading to a temporary driver suspension). — [MustShareNews](https://mustsharenews.com/ryde-passenger-cancelled-trip/)
- **COMPARATOR (TADA, not Ryde)** [FETCHED], Mothership, 12 March 2026: from 18 March 2026, 12pm, TADA drivers get S$3 if a ride is cancelled after they have travelled toward the pickup for at least three minutes and are within about 500m of it; previously S$3 applied only when a passenger cancelled or failed to turn up after the driver arrived. — [Mothership: Tada cancellation and cleaning fee](https://mothership.sg/2026/03/tada-cancellation-cleaning-fee/)
- **COMPARATOR (TADA, not Ryde)** [SNIPPET]: a rider who cancels on a driver en route for at least three minutes and within 500m of the pickup pays $2.50. — [Yahoo News Singapore](https://sg.news.yahoo.com/tada-revises-cancellation-fee-policy-014031841.html)
- **COMPARATOR (Grab, not Ryde)**: headline only, not opened: "Grab Singapore slashes waiting period, cancellation time from 5 to 3 min". — [Yahoo Finance Singapore](https://sg.finance.yahoo.com/news/grab-singapore-waiting-cancellation-5-3-minutes-084203187.html). ### Inferences
- With 0% commission on PHV/taxi trips (section 4), the cancellation/waiting fee appears to pass to the driver in full; the help excerpt says the fee is "credited to the driver" and nothing found says Ryde takes a cut. This is an inference, not a stated rule.
- A no-show dispute on Ryde turns on three timestamps: match time, "I'm Here" press, and cancellation time. The 3-minute thresholds are the testable facts.

### Gaps
- The $4.50 amounts could not be verified by direct read, and whether they differ by service tier (RydeX / XL / LUXE / PET) is unknown.
- Whether the waiting fee is flat or accrues per additional block of minutes is not confirmed.
- Whether GPS proximity to the pickup is checked before "I'm Here" is accepted is not published.

## 4. Commission model and platform/driver fees (who gets disputed amounts)

### Takeaway
Ryde markets 0% commission for private-hire and taxi drivers (help centre: until 31 December 2027) and earns instead from a rider-paid platform fee, while its Terms of Use still reserve a service fee of "up to 20%". Ryde contractually positions itself as a mere collection agent and says it is not party to rider-driver disputes.

### Cited Findings
- RYDE [SNIPPET], help centre: no commission fees for PHV and taxi drivers until 31 December 2027; a 10% service fee applies to RydeSEND and to carpool drivers. — [Ryde Help: Service Fee](https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/360003567993-Service-Fee)
- RYDE (press release, January 2025): "Ryde continues to uphold its industry-leading 0% commission policy and 7 Driver Commitments"; "The 0% commission policy is extended to all private-hire and taxi drivers on the platform."; platform fee from January 23, 2025: $1.05 (exclusive of GST) for fares $18 and below and $1.25 (exclusive of GST) for fares above $18. — [Ryde press release via Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/ryde-group-maintains-0-commission-driver-partners-announces-platform-fee-adjustments) (search excerpt; not opened)
- RYDE [SNIPPET], help centre lists the platform fee as $1.25 (including GST) for fares $18 and below and $1.47 (including GST) for fares above $18. — [Ryde Help: Service Fee](https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/360003567993-Service-Fee). Note: $1.05 and $1.25 plus 9% GST would be about $1.14 and $1.36, so these are not the same figures as the January 2025 release; the help-centre numbers imply a later increase or a different basis. Unresolved.
- RYDE [FETCHED], Handbook 2024 (last updated 21 Dec 2023), older offer terms: "Sign up as a Ryde driver-partner in 2024 to qualify for a full 12-month duration of 0% Commission"; "Once your account is verified, you will commence one year of commission-free earnings from the date of verification". Superseded by the later extensions above. — [Ryde Driver-Partners Handbook 2024 (PDF)](https://rydesharing.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Ryde-Driver-Partners-Handbook-2024.pdf)
- RYDE [FETCHED], Handbook cash-out and wallet rules: Standard Cash Out "Minimum of $1, No Maximum Amount / $1 Admin Fee will be charged / Credited to your bank account within 5 working days"; Instant Cash Out "Minimum of $1, Maximum of $225 / $1 Admin Fee will be charged / Credited to your bank account immediately"; "For Cash Payments, fees will be deducted from your Wallet while you collect the full fare." — [Ryde Driver-Partners Handbook 2024 (PDF)](https://rydesharing.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Ryde-Driver-Partners-Handbook-2024.pdf)

### Inferences
- For a disputed PHV/taxi fare on Ryde, the fare component belongs to the driver in full and the platform fee belongs to Ryde. A refund of the fare therefore comes out of the driver's side unless Ryde funds it as goodwill; a refund of the platform fee is Ryde's own money. This allocation is inferred from the fee model, not stated by Ryde.
- The published Terms (up to 20% service fee, "complaints must be taken up with each other directly") are older in style than the operational help-centre practice (0% commission, Ryde-adjudicated cleaning claims). Mock clauses should follow the operational practice and cite the Terms only for the legal backstop.

RYDE: more than 3 minutes after "I'm Here" (help-centre excerpt). — [Ryde Help: Cancellation and Waiting Time Policy](https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/33362932998937-Cancellation-and-Waiting-Time-Policy). COMPARATOR Grab: "Extreme cancellation of 50% and above (All driver-partners)". — [Grab SG Driver Code of Conduct](https://www.grab.com/sg/terms-policies/code-of-conduct-driver/)
- Late cancel / no-show pay to driver. RYDE: $4.50 (excerpt, unverified). — [Ryde Help: Cancellation and Waiting Time Policy](https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/33362932998937-Cancellation-and-Waiting-Time-Policy). COMPARATOR TADA: S$3, extended from 18 March 2026 to cancellations when the driver has travelled at least three minutes and is within about 500m. — [Mothership](https://mothership.sg/2026/03/tada-cancellation-cleaning-fee/)
- Cleaning-fee cap. RYDE: "up to S$200" in Terms; S$140 and S$150 (pets) in help excerpts; $70 on an old RydePET page. — [Ryde Terms of Use](https://rydesharing.com/terms-of-use/); [Ryde Help: My Rider Made a Mess](https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/900004381243-My-Rider-Made-a-Mess); [Ryde Help: My pet soiled the driver's vehicle](https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/900003466226-My-pet-soiled-the-driver-s-vehicle). COMPARATOR TADA: maximum S$150 from 18 March 2026. — [Mothership](https://mothership.sg/2026/03/tada-cancellation-cleaning-fee/). COMPARATOR Grab: no amount in Terms; "reasonable cost" once "verified by Grab". — [Grab SG Terms of Service](https://www.grab.com/sg/terms-policies/transport-delivery-logistics/)

RYDE: clean within 24 hours, claim within 48 hours, appeal within 24 hours (excerpt). — [Ryde Help: What if the rider made a mess?](https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/900006407643-What-if-the-rider-made-a-mess). COMPARATOR Gojek: no deadline stated. — [Gojek SG Help](https://www.gojek.com/sg/help/driver/fares-and-charges/i-would-like-to-request-a-cleaning-fee)

RYDE: report via RydeHELP, return within 24 hours, recommended $25 minimum at rider's discretion. — [Ryde Driver-Partners Handbook 2024 (PDF)](https://rydesharing.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Ryde-Driver-Partners-Handbook-2024.pdf). ### Inferences
- A realistic driver-side mock policy can be assembled as: Ryde wording for pickup, no-show timing, cancellation threshold, cleaning-claim evidence, lost items and reactivation; Grab-derived wording (labelled) for route adherence and extra passengers; and clearly labelled invented provisions for physical-damage quotes and a discipline ladder, since no Singapore platform publishes those.

### Gaps
- Gojek driver conduct rules (route, cancellation thresholds, discipline) and TADA's full Community Guidelines were not retrieved; only their cleaning-fee and cancellation-fee points are covered.
- Grab's current published cleaning-fee amount and evidence requirements for Singapore were not found on Grab's own pages; figures cited are from news reports.
- No National Private Hire Vehicles Association statement specific to Ryde was found; the NPHVA material located concerns TADA.
- No Straits Times, CNA or Vulcan Post coverage specific to Ryde's damage or cleaning policy was retrieved.
